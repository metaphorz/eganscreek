About this model

The ground heights, the creek’s path, and the broad habitats come from public data. Plants and birds, flying or standing, are illustrations placed by habitat. They do not show individual trees or sighting records.

Height is stretched about ten times compared with distance, because the island is otherwise nearly flat. On the Greenway, the wetland is drawn mostly as cordgrass and the narrow creek runs just west of the paths, based on what the site’s owner has seen there. The mapped creek line runs elsewhere on parts of that stretch. Fort Clinch follows its mapped outline, but its wall height is assumed. This model is not for navigation, flood estimates, or boundaries.

Sources: elevation from USGS 3DEP; land cover from NLCD 2021; creek path from the USGS National Hydrography Dataset; roads, trails, and buildings © OpenStreetMap contributors.
